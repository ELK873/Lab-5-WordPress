Lab 5 - WordPress Persona Site
ENSE 271
By: Eildvin Logrono

For this lab I made a persona named Glenn Mangalus, a 21 year old bank teller from Kipling who saves up for concerts and gets stressed about surprise fees, long queues and resale scams.
Then I built a WordPress site in Local called Glenn's Head as if Glenn made it himself, with a home page, three static pages (My Playlist, Concert Wishlist and Ticket Habits & Gear) and a blog.
The three blog posts each cover one of his pain points with buying tickets, the drive to Regina and resale scams, waiting in the queue with no updates, and the price jumping at checkout.
The persona is in persona.png and the screenshots of every page are in the screenshots folder.
